Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-05-07 · Package com.nesdaylive.pdfmasterai

PDF Master AI (\"we\", \"our\", \"the app\") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • PDF files you import or scan are stored on your device and/or our secure backend so the app can render, edit, summarize and chat with them.
  • Text content extracted from your scans / PDFs.
  • Chat messages you send to the AI assistant.
  • Subscription / payment data processed by Stripe (we never see your card details).

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • App preferences (theme, language, auto-save).
  • Anonymous usage diagnostics (crash logs, feature usage) used solely to improve the app.
  • Device information (model, OS version) attached to crash reports.

1.3 Camera & Photos android.permission.CAMERA

  • The Camera is used only when you explicitly tap \"Scan\" to capture documents and convert them to text/PDF.
  • Captured images are processed on-device via OCR and discarded immediately after extraction unless you choose to save the resulting PDF.
  • We do not access the camera in the background.

1.4 Storage Permissions

READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE are used only to import PDFs you select and to save PDFs you export.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide the core PDF features (viewer, editor, OCR, AI chat).
  • Run AI processing through trusted providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google) under their privacy policies.
  • Process subscription payments through Stripe.
  • Improve app stability and performance.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data and do not show third-party advertising in the paid tier.

3. AI Processing

When you use the AI Assistant, the relevant document text and your messages are transmitted to the selected model provider (Anthropic Claude, OpenAI GPT, or Google Gemini) to generate a response. These providers process the data per their policies and do not use it to train their public models when accessed via API.

4. Data Retention

  • Files & chat history remain in your account until you delete them.
  • Trashed files are kept until you permanently delete them.
  • Backend logs are retained for up to 30 days for debugging.
  • You can delete all your data any time from Settings → Account → Delete data.

5. Data Sharing

We share data only with:

  • Anthropic / OpenAI / Google (AI processing)
  • Stripe (payments)
  • Service providers hosting our backend
  • Law enforcement, when legally required

6. Security

We use HTTPS in transit, encrypted-at-rest databases, and least-privilege access controls. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

PDF Master AI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (GDPR, CCPA, DPDP Act 2023, etc.), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, port your data, or withdraw consent. Send requests to support@nesdaylive.com — we respond within 30 days.

9. International Transfers

Data may be processed in countries outside your own. We use standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards where required.

10. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of material changes via in-app notice or email. Continued use after changes means you accept the revised policy.
